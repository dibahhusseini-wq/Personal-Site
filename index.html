About

I'm a Biotechnology graduate whose research centers on antimicrobial resistance (AMR) and Vibrio cholerae, with hands-on clinical placement experience at the Federal Medical Centre (FMC), Jalingo.

I'm currently serving as a corps member with the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC), while working toward graduate study and a long-term goal of leading AMR surveillance and cholera eradication initiatives in Nigeria.

Outside the lab, I write — mostly on family, society, and the things I notice in the spaces between science and everyday life.
